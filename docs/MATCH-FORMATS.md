# 10 m Air Rifle match formats: timings from the rulebooks (built in; please check against your copies)

Every time below is read from the rulebook named, with the rule number, so it can be checked against your copy.
"Pause" is the short gap while electronic targets switch from sighting to record; no target-change time is used
anywhere (electronic targets). Times are minutes unless marked.

Sources (downloaded 2026-10-26):
- **ISSF** Rule Book, 2026 edition (2025), third print, effective 19 October 2026: 6.11.1, 6.11.9.1, 6.17.2.
  USA Shooting applies ISSF rules for these events (its 2025 rule extract for NCAA quotes the same 6.11.1).
- **CMP** National Standard Three-Position Air Rifle Rules, 16th edition, valid October 2026 to September 2028:
  5.3, 7.5 to 7.11, 10. (Also the rulebook NFHS / high-school 3-position air rifle uses.)
- **NRA** Precision Air Rifle Rules, 2025: 7.1, 8.1 to 8.5, 9.2. NRA Sporter Air Rifle Position Rules (file
  without a printed year: to confirm the edition).

## Who switches a target from sighting to record

| Rulebook | Rule | Switch |
|---|---|---|
| ISSF / USAS, single position | 6.10.4 b, 6.11.1.1 j, 6.11.1.2 a | Range personnel, after "STOP" and a ~30 s pause; then "MATCH FIRING…START". Shooter cannot switch early. |
| CMP 3-position and standing | 7.8, 7.9, 7.11 | After the sighting stage "STOP", a pause to switch the targets to Match Fire, then "…MATCH FIRE…START". No early switch; no sighters in record stages (7.15.1). |
| NRA precision / sporter | 8.1, 9.2 a | Sighters "at any time after START within the time limit for that match or stage"; once the first record shot of a position is fired, no more sighters (2-point penalty each, 9.2 d). The shooter decides. |

## ISSF / USA Shooting 10 m Air Rifle (Men, Women, Juniors): qualification, 6.11.9.1 and 6.11.1

| Phase | Time | Commands / warnings |
|---|---|---|
| Called to the line | 25 before start (10 set-up) | |
| Preparation and sighting | 15, unlimited sighters | "PREPARATION AND SIGHTING TIME…START"; "30 SECONDS" at 14:30; "END OF PREPARATION AND SIGHTING…STOP" |
| Pause (targets to match) | ~30 s | |
| Match: 60 shots | 75 (1 h 15) | "MATCH FIRING…START"; "10 MINUTES" and "5 MINUTES" remaining; "STOP…UNLOAD" |
| Between relays | 15 break | |

Scoring decimal; top 8 to the Final (qualification scores not carried). Shot after STOP of P&S and before
MATCH START: not a match shot, 2-point penalty on the first competition shot (6.11.1.1 k). Shot after STOP: miss
(6.11.1.3).

## ISSF / USA Shooting 10 m Air Rifle Final, 6.17.2 (unchanged for 2025-2028)

| Phase | Time |
|---|---|
| Equipment set-up | from 20 before start |
| "ATHLETES TO THE LINE" | ~10 before start; then "TAKE YOUR POSITIONS", 30 s |
| Preparation and sighting (combined) | 5, unlimited sighters; "30 SECONDS"; "STOP…UNLOAD" |
| Targets cleared for match | 60 s |
| 2 series of 5 shots | 250 s each; "FOR THE FIRST COMPETITION SERIES…LOAD", START 5 s later; STOP at 250 s or when all have fired |
| 14 single shots | 50 s each; "FOR THE NEXT COMPETITION SHOT, LOAD", START 5 s later |
| Eliminations | after shot 12 (8th), 14, 16, 18, 20, 22 (bronze decided), 24 (silver/gold) |
| Ties for elimination | extra single shots until broken |

Announcer comments (15-20 s) between series and shots: the RO paces those. ISSF notes the timings are guidelines.

## CMP 3x10 (5.3.2) and 3x20 (5.3.3), kneeling-prone-standing order

| Stage | 3x10 | 3x20 | Warnings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Call to line | ≥5 before P&S (5.3.1 b, 7.6) | same | |
| Preparation and sighting, kneeling | 8 | 8 | 30 s |
| Pause, then kneeling record | 10 (10 shots) | 20 (20 shots) | 5 and 2 min |
| Changeover kneeling to prone | 5 | 5 | |
| Sighting, prone | 5 | 5 | 30 s |
| Pause, then prone record | 10 | 20 | 5 and 2 min |
| Changeover prone to standing | 5 | 5 | |
| Sighting, standing | 5 | 5 | 30 s |
| Pause, then standing record | 15 | 25 | 5 and 2 min |

5.3.1 d: for inexperienced 3x10 athletes the Match Director may lengthen the first P&S to 15 or 20 minutes.
Scoring: whole rings, or tenths with electronic targets as the program announces (5.3.1 e).

## CMP standing (5.3.4)

| Event | Stages |
|---|---|
| 3x20 standing | P&S 8; record 20 shots 25; sighting 5; record 20 shots 25; sighting 5; record 20 shots 25 (with electronic targets the 60-shot event "may be fired without changeover periods") |
| 60 standing | P&S 15; record 60 shots 75 |

40-shot standing events are mentioned in 5.3.1 e (scoring) but have no table in 5.3.4: to confirm.

## NRA Precision Air Rifle (7.1, 8.2), prone-standing-kneeling order

Sighting: 8 for the first set of targets or position, 5 for each one after. Record: 1 minute per shot prone and
kneeling; standing 10 shots 15, 20 shots 25, 40 shots 50, 60 shots 75. Warnings at 5 and 2 minutes (8.4).
Position change: at least 5 minutes, 15 recommended (8.2 b). Unused time does not carry over (8.1).

| Event | Order and record times |
|---|---|
| 3x10 | prone 10, standing 15, kneeling 10 (each after its sighting period) |
| 3x20 | prone 20, standing 25, kneeling 20 |
| 3x40 | prone 40, standing 50, kneeling 40 |
| 40 standing / 60 standing | 50 / 75 |

NRA Sporter (separate book) uses the same time table and also 4-position (prone, standing, sitting, kneeling)
courses: to confirm against a dated edition.

## How the formats are built in (fetserver/matchformats.py)

Each table above is one format; each phase carries its rule number. The "pause" between sighting and record is 30 s
(ISSF 6.11.1.1 j says about 30 s; CMP 7.8 / 7.11 says a pause is needed without giving a length: 30 s is our choice).
Commands spoken on the RO console are the rulebook commands. NRA 3-position: one clock per position (sighting time +
record time, 8.1) and a 15-minute position change (8.2 b, recommended; at least 5). Team sizes per rulebook are
placeholders until the team rules are read (phase 2).
