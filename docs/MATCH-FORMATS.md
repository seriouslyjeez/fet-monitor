# 10 m Air Rifle match formats: timings from the rulebooks (built in; please check against your copies)

Every time below is read from the rulebook named, with the rule number, so it can be checked against your copy.
"Pause" is the short gap while electronic targets switch from sighting to record; no target-change time is used
anywhere (electronic targets). Times are minutes unless marked.

Sources (downloaded 2026-10-26):
- **ISSF** Rule Book, 2026 edition (2025), third print, effective 19 October 2026: 6.11.1, 6.11.9.1, 6.17.2.
  USA Shooting applies ISSF rules for these events (its 2025 rule extract for NCAA quotes the same 6.11.1).
- **CMP** National Standard Three-Position Air Rifle Rules, 16th edition, valid October 2026 to September 2028:
  5.3, 7.5 to 7.11, 10. (Also the rulebook NFHS / high-school 3-position air rifle uses.)
- **NRA** Precision Air Rifle Rules, 2025: 7.1, 8.1 to 8.5, 9.2. NRA Sporter Air Rifle Position Rules (file
  without a printed year: to confirm the edition).

## Who switches a target from sighting to record

| Rulebook | Rule | Switch |
|---|---|---|
| ISSF / USAS, single position | 6.10.4 b, 6.11.1.1 j, 6.11.1.2 a | Range personnel, after "STOP" and a ~30 s pause; then "MATCH FIRING…START". Shooter cannot switch early. |
| CMP 3-position and standing | 7.8, 7.9, 7.11 | After the sighting stage "STOP", a pause to switch the targets to Match Fire, then "…MATCH FIRE…START". No early switch; no sighters in record stages (7.15.1). |
| NRA precision / sporter | 8.1, 9.2 a | Sighters "at any time after START within the time limit for that match or stage"; once the first record shot of a position is fired, no more sighters (2-point penalty each, 9.2 d). The shooter decides. |

## ISSF / USA Shooting 10 m Air Rifle (Men, Women, Juniors): qualification, 6.11.9.1 and 6.11.1

| Phase | Time | Commands / warnings |
|---|---|---|
| Called to the line | 25 before start (10 set-up) | |
| Preparation and sighting | 15, unlimited sighters | "PREPARATION AND SIGHTING TIME…START"; "30 SECONDS" at 14:30; "END OF PREPARATION AND SIGHTING…STOP" |
| Pause (targets to match) | ~30 s | |
| Match: 60 shots | 75 (1 h 15) | "MATCH FIRING…START"; "10 MINUTES" and "5 MINUTES" remaining; "STOP…UNLOAD" |
| Between relays | 15 break | |

Scoring decimal; top 8 to the Final (qualification scores not carried). Shot after STOP of P&S and before
MATCH START: not a match shot, 2-point penalty on the first competition shot (6.11.1.1 k). Shot after STOP: miss
(6.11.1.3).

## ISSF / USA Shooting 10 m Air Rifle Final, 6.17.2 (unchanged for 2025-2028)

| Phase | Time |
|---|---|
| Equipment set-up | from 20 before start |
| "ATHLETES TO THE LINE" | ~10 before start; then "TAKE YOUR POSITIONS", 30 s |
| Preparation and sighting (combined) | 5, unlimited sighters; "30 SECONDS"; "STOP…UNLOAD" |
| Targets cleared for match | 60 s |
| 2 series of 5 shots | 250 s each; "FOR THE FIRST COMPETITION SERIES…LOAD", START 5 s later; STOP at 250 s or when all have fired |
| 14 single shots | 50 s each; "FOR THE NEXT COMPETITION SHOT, LOAD", START 5 s later |
| Eliminations | after shot 12 (8th), 14, 16, 18, 20, 22 (bronze decided), 24 (silver/gold) |
| Ties for elimination | extra single shots until broken |

Announcer comments (15-20 s) between series and shots: the RO paces those. ISSF notes the timings are guidelines.

## CMP 3x10 (5.3.2) and 3x20 (5.3.3), kneeling-prone-standing order

| Stage | 3x10 | 3x20 | Warnings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Call to line | ≥5 before P&S (5.3.1 b, 7.6) | same | |
| Preparation and sighting, kneeling | 8 | 8 | 30 s |
| Pause, then kneeling record | 10 (10 shots) | 20 (20 shots) | 5 and 2 min |
| Changeover kneeling to prone | 5 | 5 | |
| Sighting, prone | 5 | 5 | 30 s |
| Pause, then prone record | 10 | 20 | 5 and 2 min |
| Changeover prone to standing | 5 | 5 | |
| Sighting, standing | 5 | 5 | 30 s |
| Pause, then standing record | 15 | 25 | 5 and 2 min |

Scoring: whole rings, or tenths with electronic targets as the program announces (5.3.1 e).

## CMP standing (5.3.4)

| Event | Stages |
|---|---|
| 3x20 standing | P&S 8; record 20 shots 25; sighting 5; record 20 shots 25; sighting 5; record 20 shots 25 (with electronic targets the 60-shot event "may be fired without changeover periods") |

## NRA Precision Air Rifle (7.1, 8.2), prone-standing-kneeling order

Sighting: 8 for the first set of targets or position, 5 for each one after. Record: 1 minute per shot prone and
kneeling; standing 10 shots 15, 20 shots 25, 40 shots 50, 60 shots 75. Warnings at 5 and 2 minutes (8.4).
Position change: at least 5 minutes, 15 recommended (8.2 b). Unused time does not carry over (8.1).

| Event | Order and record times |
|---|---|
| 3x10 | prone 10, standing 15, kneeling 10 (each after its sighting period) |
| 3x20 | prone 20, standing 25, kneeling 20 |
| 3x40 | prone 40, standing 50, kneeling 40 |
| 40 standing / 60 standing | 50 / 75 |

NRA Sporter (separate book) uses the same time table and also 4-position (prone, standing, sitting, kneeling)
courses: to confirm against a dated edition.

## Moving a shooter to another target (a target fails)

| Rulebook | Rule | What the moved shooter gets |
|---|---|---|
| ISSF / USAS | 6.10.9.2, 6.10.9.4, 6.11.3.2 | Moved to a reserve position when the target cannot be repaired within 5 minutes; when ready, 5 minutes added to the time remaining, and unlimited sighting shots before the remaining record shots. |
| CMP | 7.18.3, 7.15.1, 8.4 a-b | The time remaining when the interruption happened plus 2 minutes, and unlimited sighters; the Range Officer authorizes sighters during Match Fire. (7.18 also allows finishing on a later relay: not built.) |
| NRA | 9.2 b, 10.3.2 | Extra time equal to the time lost (interruption over 3 minutes), and unlimited sighting shots after moving to another firing point. |

How FeT Monitor does it (Match Control console, *Move…* on a firing point; range officer or match director):
- the shooter's series goes with them to the new target, so their shots so far stay theirs; the old target leaves
  the match (a shot fired on it before the move still counts, even if it arrives later);
- their own clock stops at the move (the time lost is given back) and runs again when the range officer presses
  *Shooter ready*, with the rulebook's extra time (ISSF 5 min, CMP 2 min, NRA none) prefilled and changeable;
- they are in sighting until *Start record* (console, or the shooter's own screen); a sighter after that is flagged;
- when the match moves on to the next phase before their time is up, they finish their phase on their own clock; a
  match whose time is over while someone is finishing shows "Time over: moved shooters are finishing";
- shots on the new target while they are moving are flagged ("while moving to another target");
- before the start, *Move…* only changes the target; in a competition, the competitor window (pencil) places a
  competitor on any relay that has not started, or swaps two competitors.
- **CMP 7.18, finishing on a later relay** (competitions; *Move…*, *Finish on a later relay*): the shots so far stay on
  this relay; on the later relay (one that has not started, or a new one) the shooter waits in sighting until the
  stage they left, then has the time they had left plus 2 minutes (7.18.3), unlimited sighters, and only the record
  shots still owed; then the rest of the match with everyone. Shots before their stage, or after their own time, are
  flagged. The results join both parts (relay and firing point shown as "1 · 3 → 2 · 1").

## Corrections: reviews, protests, crossfires, penalties, disqualification

| Topic | ISSF / USAS | CMP | NRA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crossfired record shot | a miss for the firer (6.11.6.1) | a miss (7.17.9) | a miss (10.10.4 a) |
| Sighter crossfired onto another's record target | 2 points off the firer's first series (6.11.6.3) | not covered | 2 points off, first series (10.10.4 a) |
| Sighter onto another's sighting target | no penalty (6.11.6.2) | no penalty (7.17.9) | no penalty (10.10.4 b) |
| Disclaimed shot | annulled if the RO confirms it; otherwise it counts (6.11.6.6-8) | the same (7.17.10) | the same (10.10.4 e-f) |
| Shot-value protest (electronic targets) | before the next shot or within 3 min; not upheld: 2 points off the shot (6.16.5.2) | the same (8.4 c) | challenges by total score (16.1) |
| Results protest | 10 min after preliminary results (6.16.5.1) | 10 min (8.2) | time set by the match (16.1 b) |
| Penalties | warning, 2 points, DSQ (6.12.6.1); false information 2 points (6.12.6.1 c) | warning, 2 points, DQ (7.22.1); DQ by two officials (7.22.2-3) | warning, deduction, DQ (9.30) |

How FeT Monitor does it (Match Control console, *Shots…* on a firing point; fetserver/corrections.py):
- range officers mark a shot *under review* or record a *protest*; the match director settles it (upheld, not upheld,
  withdrawn). A protest of a shot value that is not upheld suggests 2 points off that shot (ISSF 6.16.5.2 c,
  CMP 8.4 c), unless the shot was a zero;
- the match director can override a shot's value (the fired value stays on record and on the score card), annul a
  shot, add a shot (marked "added by the match director"), record a crossfire (from a firing point, or unknown),
  give a penalty (a rule reference is required) or disqualify (a rule reference and a second official, CMP 7.22);
- a penalty without a shot comes off the first series (its best shot, so the whole deduction applies);
- *too many shots* in one step: "Annul, 2 points off" annuls the extra shot and takes 2 points off the lowest shot of
  the first series (ISSF 6.11.5, CMP 7.17.8 a); NRA: annul the highest-value hit (10.10.4 d);
- *a shot did not register* (range officer or match director; ISSF 6.10.9.3, CMP 8.4 a): the shooter fires one more
  match shot; after the stage the match director settles it: found (its value is entered and counts) or not found
  (a miss); either way the last shot of that stage, the extra one, is annulled. Marked twice (the extra did not
  register either), the console offers Move (ISSF 6.10.9.4);
- *disturbance* (ISSF 6.11.7): justified, the shot is annulled and may be fired again; not justified, it counts;
- *double load* (CMP 7.17.11, ISSF 6.11.2.4 b): the lower of the two shots is annulled;
- nothing is deleted: every correction is kept with who and when, and can be voided;
- common rule references are explained in plain words on the results and score cards (fetserver/rules.py).

## Official results and decision guidance

- Results are *provisional* until the match director makes them *official*: only when the match finished, every
  flagged shot is decided and every review or protest is settled. Official results cannot be changed until they are
  reopened (logged). A competition is official when every relay that was not stopped is. Results pages and print-outs
  say which. The rulebook protest time (10 minutes after the results are posted, ISSF 6.16.5.1, CMP 8.2) is shown,
  not enforced.
- Each flagged shot shows the rule that applies for the match's rulebook (ISSF 6.11.1.1 h/k, 6.11.1.2 d, 6.11.1.3 a,
  6.11.5; CMP 7.15.1, 7.17.1-3, 7.17.8; NRA 9.2 d, 10.10.4 d, 14.5), a plain summary, a link to the rulebook page and
  the suggested decision, or what to do by hand where no decision fits (e.g. NRA 14.5: the highest-value shot becomes
  a miss). The suggestion is never applied by itself.

## How the formats are built in (fetserver/matchformats.py)

Each table above is one format; each phase carries its rule number. The "pause" between sighting and record is 30 s
(ISSF 6.11.1.1 j says about 30 s; CMP 7.8 / 7.11 says a pause is needed without giving a length: 30 s is our choice).
Commands spoken on the RO console are the rulebook commands. NRA 3-position: one clock per position (sighting time +
record time, 8.1) and a 15-minute position change (8.2 b, recommended; at least 5). Team sizes per rulebook are
placeholders until the team rules are read (phase 2).

## Ranking and tie-breaking (built in: fetserver/ranking.py)

| Rulebook | Rule | Order |
|---|---|---|
| ISSF / USAS, decimal (10 m air rifle) | 6.15.1 f | total; last 10-shot series backwards (decimal); shot by shot backwards (decimal); then equal rank, listed alphabetically by family name (e) |
| ISSF, whole rings | 6.15.1 a-d | total; inner tens; last 10-shot series backwards; shot by shot backwards with an inner ten above a ten; shot by shot in decimals (EST) |
| CMP, decimal | 8.6.1 e | as ISSF decimal (inner tens are not used with decimals, 8.6.1 a) |
| CMP, whole rings | 8.6.1 a-d | total; inner tens; 10-shot series backwards (score, then that series' inner tens); shot by shot backwards, inner ten above a ten; then duplicate awards |
| NRA | 15.3 | total; last 10-shot string backwards; most 10s, then 9s, 8s ...; most inner 10s; then equal rank alphabetically |

Teams: ISSF 6.15.5, CMP 8.6.4, NRA 15.7: the members' results totalled and ranked like one competitor (countback
lists added position by position).

## Teams

| Rulebook | Rule | Team |
|---|---|---|
| ISSF / USAS | 3.3.2.3, 6.11.10 | 3 athletes; the team score is the sum of their qualification scores; only full teams are ranked |
| CMP | 3.2 | 4 athletes; "Best-Four-Count" teams (3.2.2 c) may have more members, the best four count |
| NRA | 2.10-2.15, 12.3 | team size set by the match program (4 used as the default here) |

The built-in default size follows the rulebook and can be changed per competition; "best count" is optional.
